Optional add-on

An AI assistant that never misses an enquiry.

Small businesses lose work when nobody can answer because they're on a job or behind the counter. The add-on plugs into your site and handles the routine bits for you.

  • 💬
    Chat assistant for enquiries & bookingsAnswers common questions about prices, hours and areas, and takes booking requests day or night.
  • ⚡
    Instant enquiry repliesEvery form or email enquiry gets a helpful reply straight away, so customers don't go elsewhere.
  • ★
    Review requestsHappy customers get a polite nudge to leave a Google review after their visit or job.

How it works

Three simple steps. All you need to give is a short chat and your feedback.

  1. Quick chat

    We talk about your business, your customers and what you want the site to do: calls, bookings, quotes or orders.

  2. I build it

    I design and build your site and share a live preview. You tell me what to change until it's right.

  3. Go live

    We launch on your domain, and add the AI assistant if you want it. I stay on hand for updates.
